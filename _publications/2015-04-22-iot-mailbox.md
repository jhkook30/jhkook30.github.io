### ⚡️ TL;DR
An undergraduate project: a mailbox that tells your phone when mail arrives, built with Arduino sensors and an Android app.

### 👩🏻‍💻 My Role
- Co-developed the Android application and the device communication layer.
- Contributed to system design and the conference poster.

### 🧪 Methods / Data
- Arduino-based sensing of mail arrival, relayed to a paired Android client.
- Focus on low-cost components and a simple notification flow.

### 📝 Note
This is early undergraduate work, kept here as part of the record. Looking back, it was the first time I built something that moved private information out of a physical space and onto a network — a question I now approach with cryptography rather than sensors.
