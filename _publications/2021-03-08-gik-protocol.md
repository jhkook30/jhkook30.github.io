**Published:** *Diabetes Research and Clinical Practice*, volume 174, article 108756, April 2021.

[DOI: 10.1016/j.diabres.2021.108756](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.diabres.2021.108756)

### Research focus
A retrospective study evaluating a standardized glucose-insulin-potassium infusion protocol using records from Seoul National University Bundang Hospital's Clinical Data Warehouse.

### My contribution
- Managed and prepared clinical data for the retrospective analysis.
- Performed 1:1 propensity score matching in R, with 227 patients per group.
- Applied linear interpolation to blood glucose measurements and compared glucose AUC and time within the target glucose range.
